# Privacy Policy for Mirai

**Last updated:** December 17, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Mirai ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
**Mirai is a "Local First" application.** We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data, diary entries, photos, or audio recordings on our servers.

* **All Data is Local:** Every diary entry, image, and audio note you create is stored exclusively on your device's internal storage.
* **No Accounts:** We do not require you to create an account or login.
* **No Analytics:** We do not track your usage patterns or collect crash data via third-party services.

### 2. App Permissions
To function, the App requires specific permissions. These are used strictly for the functionality described below and never to transmit data off your device.

* **Camera:** Used solely to take photos that you explicitly choose to attach to a diary entry.
* **Microphone:** Used solely to record audio notes that you explicitly choose to attach to a diary entry.
* **Storage / Photos:** Used to allow you to select existing images from your gallery to add to your diary.

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion
Since we do not host your data, we have no ability to retain or delete it remotely.
* **Retention:** Data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
* **Deletion:** You can delete individual entries within the App. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all local data associated with Mirai (unless you have manually backed up the files).

### 4. Third-Party Services
This App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
